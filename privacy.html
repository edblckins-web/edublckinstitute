Privacy

Privacy Policy

Short version: no trackers, no cookies, no selling. Only what you send through the contact form.

Effective date: 1 September 2026. This policy covers edublckinstitute.com.

What this site collects

This site has no analytics, no advertising trackers, and sets no cookies. The only information collected is what you type into the contact form and choose to send.

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The topic checkboxes you select
  • The message you write
  • Whether you opted in to receive emails

Why it is collected

To reply to you. If you tick the optional box, your email may also be used to send occasional updates from EduBlck Institute. You can unsubscribe at any time, and declining does not affect whether you get a reply.

Where it goes

Contact form submissions are transmitted to Google Workspace and stored in a private Google Sheet controlled by Karla Hannah. Google acts as a service provider. Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for their own purposes.

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those files, Google receives your IP address as part of that request. No other third-party resources are loaded.

How long it is kept

Contact form entries are kept for as long as needed to respond and maintain a record of the conversation, and are deleted on request.

Your choices

You may ask what information is held about you, ask for a copy, ask for corrections, or ask for deletion. Email edblckins@gmail.com and it will be handled within 45 days. You will not be treated differently for making a request.

California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA. This site does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in that law.

Do Not Track

This site does not track visitors across other websites, so there is no behavior to change when a Do Not Track signal is sent.

Children

This site is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information here, email edblckins@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the revised policy posted on this page.

Contact

EduBlck Institute, Pittsburg, California. edblckins@gmail.com

Accessibility

This site aims to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. If any part of it is difficult to use, email edblckins@gmail.com describing the problem and the page, and it will be addressed.
